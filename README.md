# Westminster Weather

**Westminster Weather** is a legislative intelligence and forecasting tool designed to predict whether UK parliamentary bills will pass into law and estimate the timeline until their commencement.

## Overview

Navigating the UK legislative process is critical for organizations operating in regulated or policy-sensitive sectors. While tracking a bill's current stage in the House of Commons or House of Lords tells you where legislation stands today, businesses need forward-looking clarity: **Will this bill pass, and when will its provisions actually take effect?**

Westminster Weather bridges the gap between parliamentary activity and commercial strategy by providing data-driven predictions on bill passage probabilities and commencement horizons—empowering businesses to plan ahead, manage regulatory risk, and make informed decisions with actionable timelines.

---

## Core Objectives

- **Bill Passage Prediction**: Forecast the likelihood of UK bills (Government Bills, Private Members' Bills, Hybrid Bills) progressing through parliamentary stages to Royal Assent.
- **Time-to-Commencement Estimation**: Estimate expected timelines from a bill's current stage to Royal Assent and subsequent commencement (when provisions legally come into force via statutory instruments or commencement orders).
- **Business Decision Support**: Give enterprises, legal teams, and policy analysts reliable timelines to prepare compliance programmes, allocate budgets, and adjust commercial strategy ahead of regulatory shifts.

---

## How It Helps Businesses

1. **Regulatory Readiness**: Anticipate upcoming statutory obligations months in advance rather than reacting after Royal Assent.
2. **Resource & Timeline Planning**: Align product roadmaps, operational changes, and compliance investments with realistic legislative commencement windows.
3. **Risk Assessment**: Distinguish between high-probability government legislation and bills unlikely to clear parliamentary hurdles before prorogation or dissolution.
